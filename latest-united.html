Men's first team · Champions League · 3 November

Old Trafford roars again as United overturn Roma

Manchester United came from two goals behind to defeat Roma 3–2 in a dramatic Champions League night. Matias Soulé opened the scoring and Donyell Malen added a penalty before half-time. United responded after the break: Bruno Fernandes converted a penalty retake to reduce the deficit, then Matheus Cunha scored twice late on to complete the comeback. Cunha was sent off near the end, so United had to protect the lead with ten men.

Michael Carrick said he was proud of the players and supporters, but stressed that one win does not solve the wider problems. He described the comeback as an important night and called for the team to build consistency. The match also gave the crowd a moment to reconnect with the team after a difficult run.

Why it matters: The result ends a ten-match winless run in all competitions, according to match-week reporting, and offers a lift before a crucial league fixture. It does not erase the poor league form or remove the need for sustained improvement.

Coverage basis: Manchester United's 3 November match report and Michael Carrick's post-match press conference; cross-checked with Guardian match reporting.
Men's team · Manager

Carrick asks for consistency, not one-night celebrations

In his post-Roma comments, Carrick praised the response after half-time and said the team kept believing instead of allowing frustration to take over. He highlighted Bruno Fernandes' willingness to retake the penalty, Youri Tielemans' impact from the bench and Shea Lacey's energy. Carrick also warned against getting carried away: United need to turn a memorable European night into better performances over a full 90 minutes.

Our read: The next test is whether United can carry that intensity into domestic football. The Roma result is encouraging; consistency is the actual assignment.

Coverage basis: Carrick's full post-match press conference, 4 November.
Men's team · Player award

Bryan Mbeumo named October Player of the Month

Mbeumo received United's October men's Player of the Month award after scoring twice across six fixtures. His goals came in the 1–1 draw with Tottenham and the 1–1 draw at Leeds. His finish against Spurs also won the club's October Goal of the Month award. It is a positive individual recognition during a difficult period for the team.

Injury update · Confirmed

Carlos Baleba faces weeks out

Baleba suffered a hamstring injury during the Roma match and was withdrawn shortly after half-time. Carrick said on 6 November that the midfielder will miss the Aston Villa fixture and the international break, with the absence expected to last a number of weeks. The exact return date has not been confirmed.

Baleba has made seven appearances for United since arriving from Brighton in the summer. His absence reduces Carrick's midfield options at a time when the team need stability and control.

Status: Club-confirmed injury update and Carrick press-conference comments, 6 November.
Team news · Aston Villa

Lisandro Martínez suspended; Dorgu being assessed

Martínez is unavailable for the Villa match because he is serving a one-game suspension following his red card against Chelsea. Patrick Dorgu has returned to training after his hamstring problems, but Carrick said he has completed only a small amount of training and will be assessed. The club says patience is important as he returns.

At the 6 November team-news update, Luke Shaw, Manuel Ugarte, Marcus Rashford and Benjamin Šeško were also listed among players who had been sidelined. Their availability should not be assumed without a fresh update.

Status: Carrick's pre-match press conference and official team-news update, 6 November.
Opposition watch · 8 November

Aston Villa arrive after a difficult European night

United host Aston Villa at Old Trafford on Sunday. Villa were beaten 3–0 by Barcelona in the Champions League in midweek. Their league position in the pre-match preview was 10th, with three wins, three draws and three defeats. Former United players Victor Lindelöf and Alejandro Garnacho are in Villa's squad. Matty Cash is suspended, while Amadou Onana and Matteo Ruggeri were listed as unavailable.

United's task is straightforward to describe and difficult to execute: make the Roma comeback a starting point, control midfield and deliver a complete league performance.

Coverage basis: official United match preview and team news, 6 November.

Form guide · 6 November

United Women carry a ten-match unbeaten run to Villa Park

Manchester United Women are unbeaten in ten matches in all competitions, with six of those games in the Women's Super League, according to the club's 6 November preview. Eva Olid's side travel to Aston Villa on Sunday looking to extend that run and start a busy month strongly.

The team last played a 0–0 draw away to Everton at Goodison Park on 31 October. It was a disciplined defensive performance, with goalkeeper Phallon Tullis-Joyce making important saves and Maya Le Tissier helping organise the back line.

Women's match report · 31 October

Everton 0–0 Manchester United

United and Everton shared the points at Goodison Park. The match was tight, with both teams having spells of pressure. Tullis-Joyce made key interventions, while United created late opportunities through Elisabeth Terland and Le Tissier. The clean sheet extended the team's league unbeaten run to six games.

United had beaten Burnley 3–0 in the Subway Players Cup earlier in the week, making it two clean sheets across the two matches.

Under-18s · Match report · 31 October

Blackburn 1–4 Manchester United: young Reds win with ten men

United's Under-18s came from behind and won despite finishing with ten players. Kai Rooney equalised after Charlie Fletcher had put Blackburn ahead. Rooney was later sent off, but Jaume Camacho Sidos scored twice and Noah Ajayi added a fine goal to complete a 4–1 win. The result made it three consecutive U18 league wins, with 14 goals scored across that run.

The report also highlighted the team's composure after going down to ten. Their next listed assignment was an U18 Premier League Cup group match against Wolverhampton Wanderers.

Coverage basis: Manchester United official U18 match report, 31 October.

In the recent run before Blackburn, United's U18s recorded a 6–0 win over Burnley and a 6–3 victory against Liverpool. Ownership, finance & governance

Accountability desk
Ownership · Reported comments

Avram Glazer's remarks reignite supporter criticism

Comments attributed to Avram Glazer at a Deutsche Bank sports-investment conference in September became a major story on 5 November. Reports said he argued that Manchester United's commercial value is resilient even when results on the pitch are poor, drawing comparisons with major American sports franchises. Supporters and commentators criticised the remarks as disconnected from the importance of winning and the match-going experience.

The comments are reported remarks, not a new ownership announcement. They do not change the club's ownership structure by themselves. Our position remains that supporters deserve transparency, accountability and meaningful representation in decisions about the club's future.

Coverage basis: reporting by talkSPORT and other UK football media on 5 November, concerning comments made at a September conference.
Annual results · Published 23 September 2026

Record revenue, operating profit — but a £43m net loss

Manchester United PLC reported full-year revenue of £677.6 million for the year ended 30 June 2026, up from £666.5 million the previous year. The club reported an operating profit of £22.6 million, compared with an operating loss of £18.4 million in 2025. However, the reported net loss was £43.0 million, compared with £33.0 million the year before.

Commercial revenue was £317.3 million, down 4.8 per cent; broadcasting revenue was £206.8 million, up 19.6 per cent; and matchday revenue was £153.5 million, down 4.2 per cent. Adjusted EBITDA was £216.4 million. The company also reported $775 million in non-current borrowings and a £110 million revolving credit facility balance at 30 June 2026.

What the figures mean: record turnover does not mean the club made a net profit. Operating profit, adjusted EBITDA and net profit/loss are different measures and should not be mixed up.

Coverage basis: Manchester United PLC full-year FY2026 results announcement, 23 September 2026. Figures are company-reported; adjusted EBITDA is a non-IFRS measure.
Supporter campaign · Permanent mandate

The Glazer ownership question stays open

UNITED BACK TO THE PEOPLE's permanent supporter question is whether the Glazer family should continue as Manchester United's controlling owners. The options are yes, no, or undecided. The first campaign milestone is 10,000 responses, after which the initiative intends to publish a transparent results report and a proposal for next steps.

That milestone is a campaign target, not a legal vote and not a mechanism that forces a sale. Responses must be collected transparently, privacy protected and limitations clearly explained.

Rumour control
January window · Editorial policy

No invented targets. No fake "done deals".

Transfer coverage is included when there is credible reporting, a direct club statement, a player's confirmation or a clearly attributed journalist report. A rumour repeated by several social accounts is not automatically reliable. At the time of this update, this newsroom is not presenting an unconfirmed January target as a completed signing.

When a story develops, we will identify the player, the clubs involved, the type of report (interest, enquiry, bid, agreement or completed transfer), the source and what remains unknown. We will not confuse a transfer rumour with official confirmation.
