Men's first team · Match report

United fight back to beat Roma 3–2

On 3 November, United recovered from two goals down at Old Trafford. Bruno Fernandes scored from the penalty spot and Matheus Cunha struck twice, including the late winner. Cunha was sent off late on, but United held on.

Head coach · Developing story

Carrick heads into a major Villa test

Michael Carrick remains in charge going into Sunday's match. His position has been questioned in media reporting during United's difficult run, but reports about possible replacements are not a club announcement. The Roma comeback gives the team a chance to build momentum.

Injury update · Confirmed by Carrick

Baleba expected to miss several weeks

Carlos Baleba suffered a hamstring injury during the Roma match. Carrick said on 6 November that the midfielder would be out for a number of weeks, ruling him out of the Aston Villa game and the international break.

Dorgu making progress

Patrick Dorgu has returned to some training after his hamstring problem and could be in contention for Villa, but his involvement is not confirmed. Check official team news before treating him as available.

Academy watch

Shea Lacey makes a European impact

The 19-year-old academy graduate assisted Cunha's winner against Roma. United report that he became the youngest Red to assist a Champions League winner at Old Trafford.

Women's first team

Maya Le Tissier wins October Player of the Month

The captain received 52 per cent of the fan vote after starting all six matches in an unbeaten October. United also named Ella Toone's strike at Charlton as Goal of the Month. The team are scheduled to visit Aston Villa on 8 November.

Ownership · Reported comments

Avram Glazer's commercial-value remarks draw criticism

Reports published on 5 November said Avram Glazer told investors that United's commercial strength is not dependent on results on the pitch. The remarks were reportedly made at a September sports-investment conference and drew criticism from supporters and commentators. This is reported commentary, not a new ownership announcement.
