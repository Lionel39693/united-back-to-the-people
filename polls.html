Permanent ownership poll · Programme 05

The Ownership Mandate

OUR CLUB. OUR VOICE. OUR FUTURE.

This is the campaign's permanent public question. It is separate from weekly football debates and will remain open until the first milestone of 10,000 responses is reached, then the campaign will publish its results and next-step proposal.

Should the Glazer family continue as Manchester United's controlling owners?
  1. Yes — they should remain as controlling owners.
  2. No — Manchester United needs a change in controlling ownership.
  3. Undecided — I need more information before choosing.

One response per person is the preferred standard. If the form requires Google sign-in, that must be disclosed. If it is open without sign-in, duplicate responses cannot reliably be prevented. We will report the actual response count and the collection method, not claim that every response is a verified supporter.

First milestone: 10,000 responses

The permanent ownership poll stays open until it reaches 10,000 responses. This is a campaign mobilisation milestone, not a legal threshold and not a mechanism that forces an ownership change.

At the milestone, publish a transparent report with the exact question, opening and closing dates, response count, voting settings, duplicate-control limits, results by option, and the limitations of an open online consultation. Then publish a peaceful, evidence-led next-step proposal for supporter groups, club representatives, shareholders and media.

We will not inflate the total or describe responses as unique or verified unless the form settings genuinely support that claim. Reaching the target does not itself transfer ownership or compel the club to act.

How we handle responses

  • Each form will display its exact question, opening and closing dates, and response method.
  • We will publish response counts and results only after checking the form's data.
  • An open online consultation is not a formal vote of all Manchester United supporters.
  • We will not claim anonymity, one-person-one-vote or verified supporter status unless the form settings genuinely provide it.
  • Do not submit phone numbers, home addresses, school details, passwords or other private information.
  • Ideas and comments are moderated. Abuse, threats, discrimination and personal allegations will not be published.
