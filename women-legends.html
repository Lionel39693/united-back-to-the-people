Women’s Legends & Greats

A separate archive celebrating the players who founded, built and continue to shape Manchester United Women since the team’s 2018 reformation.

Independent historical archive. Profiles are written for supporters and are not official Manchester United biographies. Career statistics are included only where the source and scope are clear; check linked records for updated totals. Current and developing players are not automatically labelled legends.
