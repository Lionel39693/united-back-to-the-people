Women’s Legends & Greats

A separate archive celebrating the players who founded, built and continue to shape Manchester United Women since the team’s 2018 reformation.

Independent historical archive. Profiles are written for supporters and are not official Manchester United biographies. Career statistics are included only where the source and scope are clear; check linked records for updated totals. Current and developing players are not automatically labelled legends.
2018–2021

Lauren James

Forward

A gifted young attacker who came through United’s youth pathway and made senior appearances before moving on to Chelsea.

United legacy: One of the early academy talents to progress into the senior side; later became a major England international.

James progressed through United’s youth pathway and made senior appearances during the team’s relaunch years. Her close control and ability to beat defenders stood out before she moved to Chelsea in 2021, where she became an established England international.

2018–2021

Jess Sigsworth

Forward

A prolific forward in the early years of the reformed team, helping United win promotion from the Championship.

United legacy: A key scorer in the inaugural 2018/19 campaign.

Sigsworth joined from Doncaster Rovers Belles for United’s first season after the 2018 relaunch. She scored 14 league goals in the 2018/19 Championship campaign, helping the club win the title and promotion with her movement and penalty-box instincts.

2018–2020

Mollie Green

Midfielder

An academy graduate who featured in the inaugural senior squad and helped establish the team’s midfield.

United legacy: Part of the original group that won the Championship in the club’s first season.

Green was part of the original senior squad assembled for United Women’s return in 2018. She contributed in midfield during the inaugural season and was part of the group that won the 2018/19 Championship and secured promotion.

2021–2023

Maria Thorisdóttir

Defender

A Norwegian international defender who added experience and versatility to the back line.

United legacy: Part of the team’s early WSL development and cup-final campaigns.

The Norwegian international joined from Chelsea in 2021, adding versatility and experience to the defence. She could play centrally or at right-back, made more than 30 appearances and was part of United’s development into a regular WSL contender.

2019–2022

Aoife Mannion

Defender

A composed defender who brought positional awareness and technical ability to United’s back line.

United legacy: Part of the squad that grew from Championship winners into WSL contenders.

Mannion joined from Manchester City in 2020 as a composed defender comfortable centrally or at full-back. Her positional awareness and experience helped strengthen the squad during its early WSL seasons before she moved on in 2022.

2021–2023

Martha Thomas

Forward

A mobile striker who contributed goals and movement during the team’s early WSL seasons.

United legacy: Part of the squad that reached the 2023 FA Cup final.

Thomas arrived from West Ham in 2020 after developing in Scotland and England. A strong centre-forward with intelligent movement, she scored important goals and was part of the squad that reached the 2023 FA Cup final before moving to Tottenham.

2020–2023

Lucy Staniforth

Midfielder

An experienced midfielder whose passing and work rate strengthened United’s options in the centre of the pitch.

United legacy: Helped the team establish itself in the WSL.

Staniforth joined from Birmingham in 2020 with England experience and a reputation for passing range and set-piece delivery. She added creativity and composure in midfield during United’s early WSL seasons before departing in 2022.

2020–2023

Ivana Fuso

Forward · Midfielder

A versatile attacker who represented United during the early years of the reformed team.

United legacy: Part of the club’s first WSL-era squads.

Fuso joined in 2020 as a young forward with experience in Germany and Switzerland. Born in Brazil and raised in Germany, she represented Germany at youth level, made senior appearances for United and later gained further experience through loan football.

2020–2023

Carrie Jones

Forward

A young Welsh international who gained senior experience during United’s formative WSL years.

United legacy: One of the emerging players in the club’s early women’s programme.

Welsh international Jones joined in 2020 as a young attacking player. Her pace and direct running offered an option out wide, while loan opportunities helped her gain senior experience and develop her place in the Wales setup.

2018–2024

Katie Zelem

Midfielder · Captain

A founding member of the reformed team who became its captain and a key set-piece taker.

United legacy: Inaugural squad member; first United Women Player of the Year in 2018/19.

Zelem returned to her hometown club in 2018 and became captain of the relaunched team. She led United to the 2018/19 Championship title, organised midfield and delivered set pieces, leaving in 2024 after more than 100 appearances as a foundational leader.

2018–present

Ella Toone

Attacking midfielder

An academy graduate whose movement, goals and creativity made her one of the defining players of the modern team.

United legacy: Scored for England in the Euro 2022 final; a central figure in United’s rise.

A United academy graduate and member of the 2018 senior squad, Toone developed into the team’s creative heartbeat. She became an England star, scoring in the Euro 2022 final, while her goals, vision and work-rate made her one of United’s defining modern players.

2018–2024

Mary Earps

Goalkeeper

A commanding goalkeeper whose shot-stopping and leadership helped establish United Women at the top level.

United legacy: England’s Euro 2022-winning goalkeeper; FIFA Best Women’s Goalkeeper in 2022 and 2023.

Earps joined from Wolfsburg in 2019 and became United’s first-choice goalkeeper. Her reflexes and command helped the team grow; she won The Best FIFA Women’s Goalkeeper award in 2023 and was central to England’s World Cup run before leaving United in 2024.

2018–2024

Leah Galton

Winger

A founding squad member whose pace, direct running and work rate made her a major attacking threat.

United legacy: One of the core players who helped build the modern team.

Galton joined in 2018 after playing in England and the United States. Her pace and direct dribbling made her a major attacking threat in the Championship-winning side, and she remained an important WSL forward through the club’s rise to the 2023 FA Cup final.

2018–present

Millie Turner

Centre-back

A founding member and long-serving defender whose aerial strength and consistency helped build the team’s defence.

United legacy: Part of the inaugural 2018 squad and the 2024 FA Cup-winning side.

Turner joined from Bristol City in 2018 and became a cornerstone of the defence. Strong in the air and composed in possession, she helped United win promotion in 2019 and reach the 2023 FA Cup final, becoming one of the club’s longest-serving players.

2018–2023

Kirsty Hanson

Forward

A founding member known for direct attacking play and hard work across the front line.

United legacy: Part of the inaugural squad that won promotion in its first season.

Hanson joined from Sheffield United in 2018 and brought pace, direct dribbling and energy to the forward line. She contributed to the Championship-winning promotion campaign and later continued her career after loan spells.

2018–2019

Alex Greenwood

Defender · First captain

The first captain of the reformed Manchester United Women, bringing experience and leadership to a new squad.

United legacy: Led the team in its inaugural season.

Greenwood joined in 2018 as captain of the inaugural squad, bringing England experience and left-footed quality. She helped United win the Championship before moving to Lyon in 2019, later becoming a leading England international and major trophy winner.

2018–2019

Siobhan Chamberlain

Goalkeeper

An experienced England international who helped provide leadership and stability to the newly formed side.

United legacy: Part of the original 2018 squad.

Chamberlain joined in 2018 with extensive WSL and England experience. Her organisation and professionalism helped guide the newly assembled squad through its first season and the Championship title-winning campaign; she later moved into football broadcasting.

2018–2021

Amy Turner

Defender

A reliable defender from the inaugural squad who helped establish the team in the Championship and WSL.

United legacy: Part of the team that won the 2018/19 Championship.

Turner was part of United’s original 2018 squad, adding defensive depth and versatility during the club’s return to senior women’s football. She featured in the team that won the inaugural Championship campaign and earned promotion in 2019.

2020–2023

Alessia Russo

Forward

A technically gifted striker whose movement, link play and finishing made her a fan favourite.

United legacy: Helped United reach the 2023 FA Cup final and qualify for the Champions League.

Russo joined from North Carolina Courage in 2020 and became a leading centre-forward through her movement, hold-up play and finishing. She scored 26 goals in 59 appearances, reached the 2023 FA Cup final and became an England star before joining Arsenal.

2020–2023

Ona Batlle

Full-back

An outstanding attacking full-back whose pace, technique and defensive quality made her a standout performer.

United legacy: Named United Women Players’ Player of the Year in 2022/23.

Batlle arrived from Levante in 2020 and quickly became one of the WSL’s most exciting full-backs. Her speed and technical ability made her a major attacking outlet; she earned PFA Team of the Year recognition before returning to Barcelona in 2023.

2019–2022

Jackie Groenen

Midfielder

A technically strong midfielder who brought energy, ball-carrying and international experience.

United legacy: Part of the team’s early WSL development.

The Netherlands international joined from Frankfurt in 2019, bringing mobility, pressing and technical quality to midfield. She helped United establish itself in the WSL before moving to Paris Saint-Germain in 2022 and continuing her international career.

2021–2023

Vilde Bøe Risa

Midfielder

A composed Norwegian international who added passing range and midfield control.

United legacy: Helped United compete near the top of the WSL.

The Norwegian midfielder joined from Sandviken in 2021, offering intelligent positioning and a strong passing range. She made appearances in league and cup competitions during her two seasons at United before continuing her career in Spain.

2021–2024

Hannah Blundell

Full-back

A dependable full-back who contributed on both sides of the pitch.

United legacy: Part of the squad that reached the 2023 FA Cup final.

Blundell joined from Chelsea in 2021 with experience of domestic trophies and European competition. A dependable full-back, she became a regular and helped United reach the 2023 FA Cup final through disciplined defending and energetic support in attack.

2022–present

Maya Le Tissier

Defender · Captain

A composed defender whose consistency and leadership quickly made her central to the team.

United legacy: Became club captain and helped lead United to its first major women’s trophy.

Le Tissier joined from Brighton in 2022 as one of the WSL’s most promising young defenders. Her calm passing and reading of the game made her a regular and later captain; she also established herself in the England squad.

2022–2024

Grace Clinton

Midfielder

An energetic attacking midfielder whose loan development and United performances highlighted her potential.

United legacy: Won the club’s 2023/24 Women’s Player of the Year award after her loan spell at Tottenham.

Clinton signed in 2022 and developed through loan spells at Bristol City and Tottenham. Her dynamic ball-carrying and ability to arrive in the box earned major recognition and an England place before she returned to become an important part of United’s midfield.

2022–2023

Nikita Parris

Forward

An experienced England forward who brought pace, movement and international pedigree.

United legacy: Part of the squad that reached the 2023 FA Cup final.

Parris joined from Arsenal in 2022 with extensive experience at Everton, Manchester City, Lyon and England. Her pace and goalscoring added quality to the attack, and she helped United reach the 2023 FA Cup final before departing in 2024.

2020–2021

Tobin Heath

Forward

A two-time World Cup winner whose technical skill and creativity brought world-class experience to the new team.

United legacy: One of the most decorated international players to represent United Women.

The World Cup-winning United States international joined in 2020, bringing elite technical quality and creativity. Injuries limited her appearances, but her presence raised the profile of United’s project before she returned to the United States.

2020–2021

Christen Press

Forward

A prolific United States international whose movement and finishing added quality to the inaugural WSL squad.

United legacy: Part of the team’s first WSL season.

Press arrived in 2020 as a World Cup-winning United States forward with intelligent movement and clinical finishing. She scored important goals before injury disrupted her spell in England; her signing connected United with one of the global stars of the women’s game.

2019–2024

Hayley Ladd

Midfielder

A hardworking midfielder who brought defensive discipline, energy and versatility.

United legacy: A key member of the side through its rise in the WSL.

Ladd joined from Birmingham in 2019 and became a dependable midfielder, known for work-rate, tackling and protecting the defence. She made more than 100 appearances, reached the 2023 FA Cup final and represented Wales at major international tournaments.

2022–present

Jess Park

Attacking midfielder · Winger

A creative, technically gifted attacker whose close control and movement give United options between and around the lines.

United legacy: A major young attacking talent in the modern squad.

Park joined from Manchester City in 2022 and gained senior experience on loan at Everton. A creative midfielder who can also play wide, she developed into a regular contributor and earned senior England recognition.

2023–present

Hinata Miyazawa

Midfielder

A dynamic Japanese international whose pressing, movement and technical quality strengthen United’s midfield.

United legacy: Part of the squad’s modern era and continued development.

The Japanese international joined in 2023 after winning the Golden Boot at the Women’s World Cup. Her sharp movement, pressing and technical quality added intelligence to midfield as she adapted to the WSL.

2022–present

Rachel Williams

Forward

An experienced striker whose physical presence, movement and big-match goals have helped United’s attack.

United legacy: Scored in the 2024 Women’s FA Cup final.

Williams returned to United in 2022 with extensive WSL experience and a reputation for important goals. Her strength and aerial ability gave the team a different centre-forward option, and she scored in the 2023 FA Cup semi-final.

2022–2024

Lucía García

Forward

A lively Spanish forward whose pace and pressing made her an important attacking option.

United legacy: Part of the 2024 Women’s FA Cup-winning squad.

García joined from Athletic Club in 2022 as a quick, technically gifted forward who could play across the front line. She contributed goals and assists and reached the 2023 FA Cup final before returning to Spain in 2024.

Academy graduate

Rebecca May

Midfielder

A pathway graduate who progressed through the club’s youth age groups before making her senior debut.

United legacy: A landmark example of the women’s academy pathway.

May developed within United’s wider women’s squad and goalkeeping pathway. Her first-team opportunities were limited, but her inclusion recognises the academy and squad players who contribute to training depth and the development of a professional team.

2018–2023

Emily Ramsey

Goalkeeper

A home-grown goalkeeper and one of the original squad members.

United legacy: Part of the inaugural team and a product of the club’s academy.

Ramsey came through United’s goalkeeping pathway and gained senior experience through loan spells. She represented England at youth level and continued her career elsewhere, with her development illustrating the importance of regular football for young goalkeepers.

2018–2023

Fran Bentley

Goalkeeper

An academy-developed goalkeeper who was part of the original senior group.

United legacy: One of the seven academy graduates in the inaugural 2018 squad.

Bentley developed within United’s goalkeeping setup and was part of the wider senior group during the club’s early WSL seasons. Her first-team opportunities were limited, but she contributed to squad depth and the club’s developing player pathway.
