# Men's player encyclopedia — expansion plan

## Goal
Build 500–1,000 original, useful men's player profiles covering every era of Newton Heath and Manchester United. This is a player encyclopedia, not a list that labels every player a legend.

## What counts as a profile
Each profile should include, where verifiable:
- Full name and known name variants
- Position and playing role
- United career seasons and first-team appearance status
- Career story and route into the first team
- Playing characteristics and contribution
- Key matches, milestones and honours
- Context about the era and teammates
- Verified statistics with competition scope and source/date
- Source notes and correction history

Short cameo players and reserve-only players must be labelled accurately. Do not invent biographies or turn sparse records into fictional detail.

## Scale and phases
- Phase 1: audit the existing 90 profiles; correct dates, positions, spellings and claims.
- Phase 2: add high-appearance players and major contributors across every era.
- Phase 3: cover lesser-known first-team players, early Newton Heath and inter-war players.
- Phase 4: expand toward 500–1,000, prioritising players with documented senior appearances.
- Phase 5: quality audit, duplicate-name resolution, source review and profile completeness checks.

## Reference registers
These are independent reference databases, not content to copy wholesale:
- MUFC 1878 player register: https://www.mufc1878.com/players (page reports 1,019 players)
- MUFCINFO A–Z player archive: https://www.mufcinfo.com/manupag/a-z_player_archive/legegendsexunited.html (page reports 989 players)
- MUFCINFO all-player appearance records: https://www.mufcinfo.com/manupag/all_players_all_apps/all_players_all_apps.html
- Official Manchester United club history: https://www.manutd.com/en/club/history

Use the registers to identify omissions and cross-check basic records. Write original biographies. Verify facts against official club material, reputable archives and match records. Different databases may disagree because they count competitions and appearances differently.

## Editorial categories
- Club legends
- Major contributors
- First-team players
- Early-era and historical players
- Academy graduates
- Current and developing players

A player can appear in the archive without being described as a legend.

## Publication standard
No profile goes live with invented stats, unsupported claims or copied biographies. If a fact cannot be confirmed, omit it or mark it as uncertain. Record the date of the latest review.
