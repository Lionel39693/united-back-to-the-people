**Campaign:** UNITED BACK TO THE PEOPLE

## Public description

UNITED BACK TO THE PEOPLE is an independent, international Manchester United supporters' initiative. We want meaningful supporter representation, transparent leadership, responsible financial stewardship and a credible long-term path towards supporter ownership. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by Manchester United Football Club.

## Launch post — publish only after voting is tested

Manchester United belongs to its history, its people and the generations who carry it forward.

UNITED BACK TO THE PEOPLE is an independent supporters' initiative calling for transparency, accountability and a meaningful voice for supporters in the club's future.

Our permanent consultation asks one clear question:

**Should the Glazer family continue as Manchester United's controlling owners?**

We want supporters from every country to read the question, consider the options and make their own choice. This is an open online consultation—not an official club ballot and not a verified vote of every United supporter.

## Launch rules

- Do not say voting is open until the production endpoint accepts a test response and aggregate results update.
- Do not claim the campaign is trending, has mass support, represents all fans or has a particular number of supporters without verifiable evidence.
- Do not buy followers, spam replies, tag unrelated accounts repeatedly or coordinate harassment.
- Invite supporters to share their own views freely; do not pressure anyone to vote a particular way.
- Publish the question, options, dates, response total, duplicate-control method and limitations.
- Never publish raw voter records or collect phone numbers, home addresses, school details or passwords.
- A trusted adult should review and supervise public-facing campaign activity and the data-handling arrangements.
- Use official club, league and reputable media sources for factual claims; correct mistakes openly.
