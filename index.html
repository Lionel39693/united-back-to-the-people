WHY WE EXIST

THE PEOPLE MUST MATTER.

UNITED BACK TO THE PEOPLE is an independent international movement uniting Manchester United supporters to advocate for meaningful change. We believe in transparency, accountability, responsible ownership and genuine supporter representation. Our purpose is to connect fans across the world and give them a stronger collective voice in the future of our club.

WHAT WE STAND FOR

OUR PRINCIPLES.

01. SUPPORTER REPRESENTATION

Supporters deserve meaningful representation in decisions that shape the club's future.

02. TRANSPARENCY

We advocate for openness, honest communication and greater clarity in club leadership.

03. ACCOUNTABILITY

Those entrusted with the club's future must be accountable for their decisions.

04. GLOBAL UNITY

United supporters everywhere deserve a voice, regardless of geography or background.

ONE CLUB. MILLIONS OF SUPPORTERS. ONE COLLECTIVE VOICE.
